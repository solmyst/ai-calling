## Booking

**How do I book?**  
Select car type and package, review inclusions, choose time slot and address (must be serviceable in Gurgaon), complete payment.

**Do prices change by car?**  
Yes. Add/select car first; price = sum of service selling prices for that car’s tier.

## Presence & prep

**Do I need to be present?**  
Needed at start and end; recommended to stay. If you step away, stay reachable by phone. Park at final spot, remove valuables, inspect before taking keys back.

**Will the professional bring equipment?**  
Yes — tools and cleaning solutions. Customer provides water access (FAQ suggests ≥2 buckets) and space around the car.

**Will cleaners drive my car?**  
No. They will not start, drive, or move the vehicle.

## Reschedule & cancel

**Can I reschedule?**  
Yes — use the in-app **Reschedule** button **up to 3 times**, while more than **2 hours** remain before slot start. Older plan copy saying “not allowed” is outdated, as is the earlier once-only limit. Ops/dashboard can reschedule without a limit.

**Can I cancel?**  
There is **no self-serve cancellation offering**. Exceptions may be granted via **Help & Support**, typically escalated to **Ops Manager** (common case: unserviceable address).

## Service limitations

Excluded examples: pet hair deep removal, biohazards, underbody, engine bay (bonnet), inner roof cleaning, seat dry-cleaning / deep fabric stains (see plan exclusions).

## Why Premium?

Goes beyond basic wash: deeper sticky-dust interior work, cladding restore, foam + wax + polish for showroom-like finish. Duration 110 mins.

## Water

Foam/premium chemistry aims for effective clean with less water than many local hose washes; customer still must provide water access.

## Subscription

**What is subscription / multipack?**  
Pay for **4** Full Car Spa Premium washes (tier from last booking car, or **T5** if none) and get **5** redemptions of **any** spa plan on **any** car tier, valid **1 year**; unused washes expire with **no refund**. ~₹2,000 is a rounded approximate (e.g. Creta ≈ ₹2,304). Details: [subscriptions.md](subscriptions.md).

## Geography

**Where is this available?**  
Gurgaon only, inside the live service polygon. See [../data/cities.md](../data/cities.md).
