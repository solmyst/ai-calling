# Product business model

**Purpose:** How the product creates and captures value (product lens).  
**Last updated:** 2026-07-14  
**Status:** Populated  
**Source:** Handbook + grill session

## Value proposition

Convenient, high-quality doorstep car spa vs going to a workshop or unreliable local washers — foam wash, detailing, before/after results, verified staff.

## Monetization

| Stream | How it works | Current note |
|--------|--------------|--------------|
| One-time bookings | Pay per plan for vehicle tier | Core volume (~20/day) |
| Subscriptions | 5 Premium washes for price of 4 / year | Live rule exists; ~0/day now |
| Add-ons | Optional ADDON SKUs (priced by tier) | **Not live** in customer app today (DB rows only) |
| Referrals / partners | Partner / referral incentives | In-app: **50% cashback**; offline: ₹200 **each side**, customer first-time only |

## Unit economics inputs (current)

| Input | Value |
|-------|-------|
| ASP | ~₹500 |
| Consumables / wash | ~₹55 |
| Kit CapEx | ~₹17,000 |
| Cleaner salary + incentives | ~₹20k–₹25k / month (rough) |
| Exact CM formula | **Not tracked** today |

## Cost drivers

- Cleaner labour & utilisation
- Consumables
- Travel / routing inefficiency (low density)
- Kits & equipment
- Marketing / CAC
- Partner incentives

## Constraints

- Fulfilment capacity (~44/day with 11 cleaners) caps growth until manpower + routing improve.
- Serviceable only inside Gurgaon polygon.
- Customer must provide water access and working space.
